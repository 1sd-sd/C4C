# Calculus & Linear Algebra — Homework 3

## Part A: Calculus

Problem 1. Find the derivative of $x^3 + 2x^2 - 5x + 1$.

Problem 2. Evaluate the integral $\int x^2 \sin(x) dx$.

Problem 3. Compute the limit $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x}$.

Problem 4. Find the derivative of $e^{x^2} \cdot \ln(x)$.

Problem 5. Evaluate $\int_0^1 \frac{1}{1+x^2} dx$.

## Part B: Equations

Problem 6. Solve the equation $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$.

Problem 7. Solve $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6 = 0$.

## Part C: Linear Algebra

Problem 8. Compute the determinant of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$.

Problem 9. Find the eigenvalues of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$.

## Part D: Differential Equations

Problem 10. Solve the ODE $y' + 2y = e^{-x}$.
